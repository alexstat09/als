## FACTS (Loop 2)
- Βούλγαρης wore the τζουμπές (ankle-length eastern robe) and was nicknamed «Τζουμπές» for it →
  https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Τζουμπές. Hydriot (red fez + white moustache = portrait features).
- Δημήτριος Γρίβας: army officer (led the Nauplia rising, Feb 1862) → uniform.
  https://military-history.fandom.com/wiki/23_October_1862_Revolution
- Δεληγιώργης (1829–79): Western dress, beard in the engravings of the 1870s (photodentro.edu.gr/cultural/r/8526/4747).
- Γεώργιος Α΄ arrived in Oct 1863, aged 17, Danish navy-trained → naval uniform; the blond moustache is his later portrait
  look (one design for every video).
- Τρικούπης proposed the principle publicly in 1874 (newspaper); voted 1875 (book). Newspaper shown without a title.
- Ballot balls (Σύνταγμα 1864): a tin box per candidate, inside split in two — RIGHT white «ΝΑΙ», LEFT black «ΟΧΙ»; one
  lead ball, raised hand, hand into the tube → https://www.sansimera.gr/articles/534
- Map S25: kingdom 1832–1864 = south of the Arta–Volos line + Euboea, N. Sporades, Cyclades; Ionian Islands still
  British until May 1864, Crete/Thessaly/Epirus/Macedonia Ottoman → SAND. The border is drawn as an approximate line
  (clip ring), and the map makes no other claim. No names on the map besides «ΟΘΩΜΑΝΙΚΗ ΑΥΤΟΚΡΑΤΟΡΙΑ» (book).
- England's two big parties in the 1870s (Conservatives/Liberals) → two facing benches; John Bull = personification.
- On-screen numbers: 1862, 1862-1864, 1875, «2» (δύο ολόκληρα χρόνια) — all in the text. No counters.
