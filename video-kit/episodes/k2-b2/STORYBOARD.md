- **ENC `#3E9C94`** αγγλικό κόμμα · **FRC `#8A5BB0`** γαλλικό κόμμα · **RUC `#D9822B`** ρωσικό κόμμα (as k2-b1/b3).
- RED = decline/violence/conflict · GREEN = satisfied/ticked · GOLD = the King/throne · grey = withered party.
- Greece flag = `grland` (land flag, cross) on buildings; Great Britain / France flags on their ships.

## Allowed numbers on screen
ONLY **1844, 1864, 1846, 1847** (the book's numbers). «πέντε από τα επτά υπουργεία» is shown as 7 doors / 5 portraits — no digits.
