## Colour meaning in this episode
- **OTTOMAN** = crimson `#B8323A` (flag: red field + white crescent, NO star — valid for the whole 1715–1821 span;
  the star was added on naval flags only in 1793 and the national flag only in 1844).
- **PATRON** = gold `#D8A93B` accents (sash / node ring / platform). Patrons sit HIGHER on screen (literal «υψηλότερη θέση»).
- **The two Peloponnesian networks** = TEAL `#3E9C94` and PURPLE `#8A5BB0` (neutral, not used for any modern faction).
- Clients / ordinary people = earthy browns, white shirts. Map land = SAND (no political colouring — see FACTS).
- RED `#DE5B45` only for ✕ / negation (same as ενότητα 4: danger/negative).

## FACTS (Loop 2)
- Ottoman flag 1715–1821: crescents on red banners throughout; 1793 Selim III naval red flag with crescent + 8-point star; national
  flag (5-point star) only 1844 → we draw red + white crescent only. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flags_of_the_Ottoman_Empire
- Fez: army 1827, officials 1829 (Mahmud II), replacing the turban → before 1821 officials wear TURBANS. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fez_(hat)
- Prokritoi/κοτζαμπάσηδες dressed in Turkish furs, καλπάκι. https://www.ethnos.gr/greece/article/26795/1821kotzampashdesenantionoplarxhgon
- Peloponnese: Ottoman again from 1715 (from Venice) to 1821 → the book's 1715–1821.
- Map makes NO claim about political control (all land SAND): the Ionian islands were never Ottoman in this period (Venetian until
  1797), so colouring «the Ottoman Empire» would be wrong. Only the three regions the book names are highlighted.
- The two Peloponnesian networks are drawn interlocking, with no territorial split (the book gives none).
- Islands: ships drawn near Hydra, Spetses, Psara (the big ship-owning islands) — no names on screen (the book names none).
- On-screen numbers: only 1715-1821 and 1821 (both in the text). No digit counters, no numbered reasons.
