Stop the early waking›
Total darkness: contoured mask + blackout curtains. Dawn light is what pulls you up. Biggest single fix.
Cool all night: AC 18°C, don't let it timer off.
Earplugs for the dawn noise.
No fluids ~90 min before bed.
Low-dose melatonin: high doses can themselves cause early waking.
Supplements›
Magnesium bisglycinate: 2 tabs in the evening. Keep, nightly.
Melatonin: 0.5–1mg (not 3–10mg). Use it to lock the schedule for 2–3 weeks, then only occasionally.
Ashwagandha: daily 300–600mg KSM-66, or not at all. Random use does nothing.
When you wake early›
Do not check your phone or the time. One glance of screen light ends any chance of drifting back.
Stay flat, eyes closed, mask on. Don't get up, don't decide you're awake.
Don't stress: "I don't have to sleep, just rest here in the dark." You'll usually slide back under.
Buy, in order›
Contoured sleep mask that stays put (~€20). Maybe your #1 fix.
Blackout curtains (~€30).
Low-dose melatonin 0.5–1mg.
Amazfit / Xiaomi smart band (~€50). Tracks deep sleep, HR, HRV, workouts. Best value, no subscription. Skip the Oura ring.

Not a sleep problem, a regularity problem. Dark, cool, and a wake time that never moves, and the hour you've never had shows up within two weeks.
